Transcriber

Whisper transcription of your audio and video files, with timestamps and SRT/VTT subtitles.

Open Transcriber in the Apify Store

What it solves

Interviews, meetings, podcasts, lectures and voice notes are useless to an agent until they are text, ideally with timestamps and subtitles, at a predictable per-minute price and without sending the audio to yet another speech API.

Transcriber runs OpenAI's Whisper (MIT) via faster-whisper on CPU inside your run. It returns the full transcript, timestamped segments (and word timings on request), the detected language with a confidence score, and SRT, WebVTT and plain-text files. Choose base, small or medium by accuracy and price.

  • 99 languages with automatic detection, or set the language; optional translation to English.
  • Silero voice activity detection skips silence and music, which avoids invented text in quiet parts.
  • MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG/Opus, WebM, MP4, MOV, MKV and more; video is transcribed from its audio track.
  • Up to 5 hours per file; long recordings are cut at quiet moments and stitched back on one timeline.
  • Your files only: links to YouTube, TikTok and other platforms are refused, free of charge.

Limits

  • 1 GB per file, 200 files per run, up to 300 minutes per file (default limit 180).
  • One audio track per file; no speaker labels (diarization).
  • Platform page links (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Spotify and similar) are refused.
  • At the default 4 GB, base transcribes about 10 minutes of audio per minute, small about 4, medium a little over 1.

Use it from an AI agent

Through the Apify MCP server, Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other MCP clients can call this Actor as a tool. Add the server with this Actor enabled:

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "apify": {
      "url": "https://mcp.apify.com/?tools=tinplatelabs/transcriber",
      "headers": {
        "Authorization": "Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN>"
      }
    }
  }
}

The agent calls it with {"media": ["https://example.com/meeting.m4a"]} and reads text (or segments for timestamps) from the dataset item, and files.srt.url for subtitles.

Or call the Apify API directly. This runs the Actor and returns the dataset items (synchronous runs time out after 300 s; for big batches start a run and read the dataset when it finishes):

curl -s -X POST \
  "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/tinplatelabs~transcriber/run-sync-get-dataset-items" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"media": ["https://example.com/meeting.m4a"]}'

Privacy

Files are processed inside your run; results go only to its dataset and key-value store. No speech-to-text API or other third party, no telemetry. See also the privacy page.

FAQ

Can I transcribe a YouTube video?

No. Transcriber works on media files you supply and have the rights to. Platform page links get a free PLATFORM_URL_NOT_SUPPORTED error. Upload the file to a key-value store or give a direct link to your own storage.

Which Whisper model should I pick?

base for clear speech and the lowest price, small for accents, noise and non-English audio, medium for the best accuracy.

Does it identify speakers?

No. There is no diarization; segments have timestamps but no speaker labels.

How do I get subtitles?

With saveSubtitleFiles on (the default), SRT, VTT and TXT files are saved to the run's key-value store and linked in files with signed URLs.

Is my audio sent to a speech-to-text API?

No. Whisper runs inside your Apify run; nothing is sent to a third party.
