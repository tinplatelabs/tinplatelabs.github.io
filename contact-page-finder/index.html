What it solves

Outreach through contact forms starts with a boring question asked thousands of times: where is this company's contact page, and does it have a form? The link is called Contact, Kontakt, Contacto or お問い合わせ, sits in the footer or behind a menu, and the form may be a WordPress plugin, a HubSpot embed or a Typeform.

Contact Page Finder takes a list of websites (or a dataset, CSV or Google Sheet) and returns, per site, the contact page URL, whether it has a contact form and which kind, the form's fields and required fields, whether it has a CAPTCHA, and the emails, phone numbers and social profiles on the contact page and home page. It never fills in or submits forms.

  • Contact page search in order: home page links scored on contact words in 20+ languages, common paths (/contact, /contact-us, /kontakt, /contacto, ...), the sitemap, a contact form on the home page.
  • Form kinds: plain HTML, HubSpot, Typeform, Google Forms, Jotform, WPForms, Contact Form 7, Gravity Forms, Elementor, Ninja Forms, Formidable, Fluent Forms, Divi, Marketo, Pardot, Salesforce Web-to-Lead, Webflow, Squarespace, Netlify, Formspree, Cognito, Formstack, Tally, Microsoft Forms and more.
  • Per form: fields (name, type, label), required fields, action, method, CAPTCHA (reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha, Turnstile, ...); HubSpot embeds give portal and form id.
  • Search, login and cart forms are left out; newsletter signups are marked as such.
  • Emails (including Cloudflare-protected ones), phone numbers and social profiles from the contact page and the home page's header and footer.
  • Websites from a list, an Apify dataset, or a CSV / JSON / Google Sheet URL, with the website column detected.
  • robots.txt obeyed with an identifying User-Agent, at most 5 pages per site by default, paced requests.

Limits

  • 10,000 websites per run; up to 15 pages per site (5 by default).
  • JavaScript is not executed: forms drawn only by scripts (some Wix, Squarespace and single-page sites) can be missed; the contact page is often still found.
  • Sites that block automated requests return a free error record.
  • Forms are never filled in or submitted.

FAQ

Does it send messages through the forms?

No. It only reads pages. What you do with the results is up to you; respect the sites' terms and anti-spam laws.

Why are the fields of a HubSpot or Typeform form empty?

Embedded forms are drawn by the provider's script after the page loads. The kind, embed URL and (for HubSpot) portal and form id are reported; open the embed to see the fields.

Why was a site not found?

Its links are probably drawn by JavaScript, or robots.txt forbids us. The record's notes list what was tried, and not-found sites are free.
